# NOTES: Buy in the Browser, and resting in a fight

README bullets to fold in (not edited in README.md itself).

## Under "The Arneyan Browser"

- Opened from a sheet, each Equipment row with a price has **Buy** beside **Add**. Buy pays the Catalogue price from that character's purse and puts the item on the sheet exactly as Add does, then says so in chat ("Aria buys Rope for 2 gp. 8 gp, 5 sp left."). Gold and platinum prices are paid gold first, then platinum, then silver (as a starting pack is); silver and copper prices are paid from the smallest coins first. If there isn't enough money it refuses and says what the item costs and what's in the purse.
- Where a price has more than one amount ("2 gp each; 10 for 15 gp", "1 sp to 10 gp"), Buy charges the first one. Items with no price (a dash, "Unique", "free (found)") have no Buy button.
- A player can Buy only for a character they own; the GM can Buy for anyone. **Add** still gives the item for free (for the GM handing out loot), and players can use it too for now.

## Under "Resting and Exhaustion"

- Resting while still in a running encounter asks first: "Aria is in a fight. Rest anyway?" with **Rest anyway** and **Cancel**. The party sheet's **Rest the party** asks the same, naming everyone still fighting.
