# Proposed effects: review list

564 proposals (feats 35, ancestries 28, class features 112, kit features 255, spells 134). Containers: passive 383, toggle 8, applies 173. Low confidence: 13.

How to read a line: **Name** (level or source, container if not passive) - what will be automated. Manual: what stays with the table. [confidence]

Conventions used:

- "First attack each round" Advantage uses adv.attack.first and applies automatically; if the text adds a further condition (vs undead, while mounted) it stays a reminder.
- Anything situational carries a "when" and shows only as a reminder on the matching roll; it is not added automatically.
- Durations: 1 minute = 10 rounds, 1 hour = 600, 1 day = 14,400; "per level" uses @cl. Dice in applied values and durations (for example 1d4 rounds) are rolled once when the effect lands; @cl in an applied effect is the caster's level.
- Ancestry size, speed, vision and attribute modifiers are assumed to come from the ancestry item fields and are not repeated as rules.
- Abilities with no number, Advantage, resistance, sense or condition to automate (pure narrative or utility, plain healing or damage) are not listed.
